About Buena Vista

Buena Vista Carwash is a family-owned business proudly serving the San Fernando community with a convenient, affordable way to keep your car looking its best.

We believe looking after your vehicle should be simple. That’s why we offer easy-to-use wash bays, self-service vacuums, and the freedom to clean at your own pace—any time of day.

As a local family business, we take pride in providing a clean, reliable, and welcoming place for our neighbors to care for their vehicles.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are you really open 24 hours?+

Yes. Our self-service car wash is open 24 hours a day, every day, so you can wash when it works for you.

How can I pay for a wash?+

We accept coins, credit card, and Apple Pay. Coin machines are available on site.

Do you wash the car for me?+

Buena Vista is a self-service car wash. You use the wash bay equipment to clean your vehicle at your own pace.

Do you sell car care products?+

Yes. Our on-site vending machines sell air fresheners in Black Ice, Coconut, New Car, Vanilla, and Strawberry, plus tire cleaner, drying towels, Armor All wipes, and Armor All spray bottles.

Do you have vacuums?+

Yes, self-service vacuums are available on site so you can freshen up your interior too.
